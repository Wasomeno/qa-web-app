# Product

## Register

product

## Users

QA engineers running test scenarios and tracking issues across projects. They work iteratively -- recording sessions, writing test cases, filing issues, and reviewing results in rapid cycles. Their context is a mix of hands-on testing and project management.

## Product Purpose

A QA testing and issue-tracking webapp that helps QA engineers organize test scenarios, record sessions, track issues across projects, and collaborate through an AI-powered agent. Success means engineers spend less time managing test infrastructure and more time finding real bugs.

## Brand Personality

Focused, modern, trustworthy. The tool should feel like a precision instrument -- capable and reliable without drawing attention to itself. Voice is direct and technical but not cold.

## Anti-references

- Bugzilla -- outdated, cluttered, visually heavy
- TestRail -- bulky, form-heavy, slow navigation
- Old-school enterprise QA tools that feel like spreadsheets with a coat of paint

## Design Principles

- **Trust through transparency** -- Make system state and data provenance clear at all times. QA engineers need to know what happened, when, and why.
- **Show, don't tell** -- Surface test status, issue state, and system context visually rather than requiring navigation or text scanning.
- **Fast by default** -- Page loads, searches, and status updates should be immediate. QA workflows are iterative; speed compounds into productivity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific requirements beyond basic web standards (semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, sufficient color contrast).
